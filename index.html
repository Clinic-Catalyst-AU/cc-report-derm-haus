Your Goals & Objectives

1. Make $5M Consistent, Profitable and Systemised

$440K a month is already above $5M annualised. The goal now is predictable acquisition, predictable conversion, predictable retention and predictable marketing return, so growing past $5M is a lever you pull, not a push.

2. More of Every Lead, Every Client and Every Marketing Dollar

At your size a few conversion points are worth more than another treatment or another $10K into ads. A landing page, a CRM and follow-up between the ad and the Timely link, so nobody who clicks and does not book simply disappears.

3. Treatment-Plan Conversion Becomes a KPI

You already send a plan after every consult. The next question is how many of those plans convert. Tracked, followed up automatically, reported weekly.

4. Bek Out of the Weeds and Into the GM Seat

One screen every Monday. AI that does for you instead of call and response. Less time in the inbox, more time with the team when you are in clinic, and present for school next year.

5. The Team Measured by Location and Practitioner

Consultations, consult to plan, plan to purchase, rebooking, retail, utilisation, revenue per treatment hour. Growth should never mean Bek does everything.
