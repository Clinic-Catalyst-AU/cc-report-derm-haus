Task One

Branding & Positioning

1. Keep what works

Your brand, your Instagram and your team are already strong, and you said it yourself: "I don't actually think we have a huge gap." We agree. So nothing on this page is about redoing your look, your content or how your team treats. What is missing sits underneath all of that: the system that catches every enquiry, follows it up, and tells you what happened. That is what we add. Everything above it stays yours.

2. Three clients, three conversations

Right now one ad is expected to speak to everyone. Your client base is three different women. The skin restoration client, 40 to 60 plus, with pigmentation, texture and ageing, who wants a proper plan and will invest in it. The preventative client, 30 to 40, already coming in for cosmetic medicine and starting to care about her skin quality, who becomes a long-term client if she is looked after now. And the acne and scarring client, a different worry, a different message, a different journey. Each one gets her own ad, her own page and her own follow-up, so every woman who sees Derm Haus feels it was written for her.

3. Concern first, machine second

A new client does not know the difference between RF needling, IPL and skin needling, and she does not need to. What she knows is "my pigmentation is getting worse" or "my skin looks tired". So the ad talks about that. It leads her to a page about her concern, teaches her a little about why it is happening and what can be done, invites her to a consultation, and the treatment plan is where the machine gets chosen, by your team, for her. You already structure part of the website this way. We take that all the way through the ads, the landing pages and the follow-up.

4. AHPRA housekeeping

AHPRA does not allow the words "expert", "expertise" or "specialist" in advertising for a clinic like yours, and it treats a website and a practitioner bio as advertising. Your practitioner bios currently use "expert" and "expertise". It is a small thing that can trigger a complaint and a takedown, and you have already lost pages once. We rewrite the bios, the site and every ad to say "has a special interest in", which is the wording AHPRA accepts, and our compliance filter checks everything that goes out from then on so it cannot creep back in.
